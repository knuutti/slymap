SlyMap

Calibration helper

Works out a map's calib for data/games.json from a few spots whose in-game position you know, then lets you click objects onto a calibrated map for its .hson data.

2. Objects (optional)

A map's own objects load with it. For a new map, load its .hson file(s) to see them on the image once there is a fit.

3. Collect objects

Click on the image to add objects. Positions come from the current fit: the points' fit, or the map's own calib. They follow the fit if it changes.

Choosing a type the map already has copies one of its objects' shape and parameters.
